Madad — Emergency SOS

Pakistan has no single 911. In an emergency, families scramble for scattered numbers — Rescue 1122, Edhi 115, police 15 — with no idea which ambulance is closest or what it costs. Madad turns panic into three taps: hold the SOS button, pick a verified ambulance with live ETA and fixed PKR fares, and get tracked to the hospital with family auto-notified. Try it in the phone — this is a working concept demo.
